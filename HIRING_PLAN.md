# LUI-1 Hiring Plan: First Founding Engineer

## 1) Hiring Objective (Why this role now)
Hire one **Founding Full-Stack Engineer** who can ship customer-facing features, harden core systems, and raise engineering velocity in the next 6 months.

**Target start date:** within 8 weeks  
**Hiring owner:** CTO (directly accountable)  
**Cross-functional partners:** CMO, UX Designer, CEO

---

## 2) Role Scorecard (Success Profile)

### Mission
Own end-to-end feature delivery from scoped requirement to production release with measurable customer impact.

### Outcomes by 6 months
1. Ship at least 3 roadmap-critical features to production with stable operations.
2. Reduce cycle time from scoped ticket to production by at least 30%.
3. Establish reliable engineering rituals (planning, peer quality checks, release checklist).
4. Improve product quality metrics (lower escaped defects, improved reliability KPIs).

### Competency scorecard
| Area | Must-Have Criteria | Evidence During Process |
|---|---|---|
| Product engineering | Can build full-stack features independently in a modern web stack | Prior shipped projects + live coding + systems discussion |
| Ownership | Proactively identifies risks and drives to resolution | Behavioral interview with concrete examples |
| Speed with quality | Ships quickly without ignoring maintainability | Past examples, code review simulation |
| Collaboration | Works effectively with design and GTM | Cross-functional panel feedback |
| Startup fit | Comfortable with ambiguity and fast iteration | Scenario interview + references |

### Non-goals (for first hire)
- Deep people management experience
- Narrow specialization only (e.g., infra-only or frontend-only)

---

## 3) Sourcing Plan (Top-of-funnel)

### Priority channels (4-week sprint)
1. **Founder/Investor network referrals** (highest signal)
2. **Targeted outbound** to early-stage startup engineers (LinkedIn + GitHub)
3. **Specialized communities** (YC Work at a Startup, relevant Slack/Discord communities)
4. **Inbound role page** with scorecard-based role description

### Weekly sourcing KPIs
- 40 outbound touches/week
- 12 qualified screens/week
- 4 onsite/final loops/week
- Time from first touch to decision: <= 21 days

---

## 4) Interview Loop (Execution-ready)

| Stage | Owner | Duration | Pass Criteria |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Recruiter/CTO screen | CTO | 30 min | Role fit, motivation, communication, logistics |
| Technical deep dive | CTO | 60 min | Architecture and implementation depth, tradeoff thinking |
| Practical build exercise | Candidate async + CTO review | 2-3 hrs max | Production-oriented code quality, clarity, tests/reasoning |
| Cross-functional collaboration | CMO + UX Designer | 45 min | Ability to partner across product/design/GTM |
| Founder values + close | CEO + CTO | 30 min | Mission alignment, ownership mindset, close readiness |

### Interview SLAs
- Feedback entered within 12 hours of each stage
- No more than 5 business days between stages
- Final decision within 24 hours of last interview

---

## 5) Decision Rubric (Weighted)

Score 1-5 per category; hire threshold: **>= 4.0 weighted avg** and no category below 3.

| Category | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Technical execution | 30% |
| Product judgment | 20% |
| Ownership and autonomy | 20% |
| Collaboration and communication | 15% |
| Startup resilience and ambiguity handling | 15% |

### Decision rules
- **Strong hire:** >= 4.3 and unanimous positive from CTO + CEO.
- **Hire:** 4.0-4.29 with no major concerns.
- **No hire:** < 4.0 or any critical risk unmitigated.

---

## 6) 30/60/90 Onboarding Plan

### First 30 days
- Environment + architecture ramp complete by week 1.
- Shadow one roadmap track, then own one scoped feature.
- Publish first engineering improvement memo (tooling/process quality gain).

### Days 31-60
- Independently deliver one roadmap-critical feature to production.
- Set up one reliability or delivery metric dashboard and baseline targets.
- Lead one planning cycle with CTO support.

### Days 61-90
- Own a full roadmap slice end-to-end (scope -> launch -> iteration).
- Document and operationalize engineering playbook v1.
- Mentor contractors/interns (if any) on code quality expectations.

---

## 7) Measurable Hiring + Onboarding KPIs

### Hiring KPIs
- Time-to-fill: <= 60 days
- Offer acceptance rate: >= 70%
- Interview-to-offer conversion: >= 20%
- Qualified pipeline health: >= 8 active qualified candidates at all times

### Post-hire KPIs (first 90 days)
- Time-to-first-production-PR: <= 14 days
- Time-to-own-feature-launch: <= 45 days
- Defect escape rate on owned work: below team baseline
- Cross-functional partner NPS (CMO/UX/CEO): >= 8/10
