# LUI-1 Hiring-Funnel Child Issue Cards (CMO)

## Child issue card 1
- Proposed title: Finalize LUI-1 Foundational Engineer Hiring Brief for Market Launch
- Department owner: CMO
- Objective: Secure a signed, outreach-ready hiring brief (role scorecard, interview loop, comp/equity range, founder narrative) so sourcing can start with a consistent quality bar this week.
- Acceptance criteria:
  - CTO scorecard non-negotiables (competencies 1, 2, 6) and pass thresholds are documented and confirmed in writing.
  - Interview loop stages, interviewer ownership, and stage rubrics are approved and shared with recruiting.
  - Compensation/equity band and guardrails are approved by CEO/CTO for candidate-facing conversations.
  - Founder narrative one-pager (mission, why now, technical scope, role leverage) is finalized for outreach usage.
  - CMO publishes a single "approved hiring brief" link to CEO, CTO, and recruiting.
- Blockers/dependencies:
  - Dependency on CTO package inputs: role scorecard, technical scope brief, interview loop, and non-negotiables.
  - Dependency on CEO approval of compensation/equity guardrails.
  - Risk: delays in cross-functional sign-off compress sourcing launch window.
- Next action: CMO schedules a 30-minute sign-off huddle with CEO/CTO today, circulates the final brief, and requests written approvals by end of next business day.
- Suggested priority: high

## Child issue card 2
- Proposed title: Launch Week-1 Candidate Sourcing and Outreach Across 3 Channels
- Department owner: CMO
- Objective: Build top-of-funnel volume fast (qualified prospects + personalized outreach) to produce first qualified screens this week.
- Acceptance criteria:
  - Prospect list of 80 qualified candidates is created and segmented (backend-leaning, frontend/full-stack, platform/product-generalist).
  - Referral request packet is sent to CEO, CTO, investors, and advisors asking for 2-3 named intros each.
  - First-wave outbound messages (minimum 60) are sent using personalized template language and 15-minute exploratory CTA.
  - Community/inbound role narrative is posted in at least 3 relevant operator/engineering communities.
  - A 10-day follow-up sequence (initial + follow-up #1 + follow-up #2) is scheduled for all outbound prospects.
- Blockers/dependencies:
  - Requires approved hiring brief from child card 1.
  - Requires up-to-date outreach templates and founder narrative for personalization.
  - Risk: low-quality targeting if must-have filters are not locked before sends.
- Next action: CMO assigns sourcing pods and ships day-1 outreach batch (minimum 20 messages) within 24 hours of brief sign-off.
- Suggested priority: high

## Child issue card 3
- Proposed title: Stand Up Weekly Funnel Operations and Candidate Throughput Cadence
- Department owner: CMO
- Objective: Run a measurable, high-velocity funnel operating cadence that converts outreach into qualified screens and founder calls this week.
- Acceptance criteria:
  - Centralized tracker/ATS view is live with stage definitions, owners, and weekly targets (prospects, outreach, response rate, screens, founder calls).
  - Operating SLAs are enforced: inbound response <=24 hours, post-interview feedback <=12 hours, next-step comms <=24 hours.
  - Monday funnel calibration sync and Tuesday/Thursday candidate standups are scheduled with required attendees.
  - At least 10 qualified screens are booked and first founder calls are scheduled from top-priority candidates.
  - Friday decision checkpoint produces a documented advance/hold/reject list and next-week action queue.
- Blockers/dependencies:
  - Depends on live candidate inflow from sourcing/outbound execution (child card 2).
  - Depends on CTO and CEO calendar availability for required syncs and first-call slots.
  - Risk: candidate drop-off if scheduling and feedback SLAs are missed.
- Next action: CMO publishes the funnel tracker and sends recurring meeting invites plus SLA ownership by close of business today.
- Suggested priority: medium
