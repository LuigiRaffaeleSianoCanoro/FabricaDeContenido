# Founding Engineer Candidate Funnel and Outreach Plan (CMO Delegated)

## 1) Hiring Goal and Execution Window

**Goal:** Generate a high-quality founding engineer pipeline and close 1 accepted offer.  
**Execution window:** Start immediately; first measurable checkpoint in 2 weeks.

**Core profile target (baseline):**
- 5-10 years building and shipping product in startup environments.
- Strong full-stack ownership (product + architecture + hands-on coding).
- Evidence of operating with ambiguity (0-to-1 builds, small team leverage, rapid iteration).
- Motivated by mission, equity upside, and direct access to CEO/CTO decision-making.

---

## 2) Sourcing Channels, Profile Targeting, and Outreach Strategy

## Channel A: Warm Network + Founder/Investor Referrals
**Target profile:**
- Senior ICs or recent staff-level engineers trusted by known founders/operators.
- Prior seed to Series A experience preferred.

**How to source this week:**
- Build a referral list from CEO, CTO, investors, advisors, and top customers.
- Request **2-3 named introductions per stakeholder** (not generic "anyone good?").

**Outreach strategy:**
- Prioritize warm intros first (highest response and close rates).
- CEO sends mission context; CTO follows with technical credibility note.
- SLA: outreach sent within 24 hours of referral.

## Channel B: Targeted Outbound (LinkedIn + GitHub + curated talent lists)
**Target profile:**
- Engineers currently owning product-critical systems at high-velocity startups.
- Public signal of shipping mindset (GitHub activity, shipping posts, product impact bullets).

**How to source this week:**
- Build list of 60-80 prospects by role keywords + startup signal filters.
- Segment into 3 pods: backend-leaning, frontend/full-stack, platform/product-generalist.

**Outreach strategy:**
- Personalized 4-touch sequence over 10 days.
- Every message includes one specific reason they were selected.
- CTA is low-friction: "15-minute exploratory call."

## Channel C: Community and Content-Led Inbound (operator communities + niche forums)
**Target profile:**
- Builders active in technical communities who value craftsmanship and ownership.
- Potentially overlooked candidates not in active job search mode.

**How to source this week:**
- Post role narrative in relevant communities (founder/engineering groups, niche Slack/Discord, HN-style forums, alumni channels).
- Ask internal team to amplify with personal context.

**Outreach strategy:**
- Use founder-authored narrative (problem, traction, why now, technical scope).
- Route all inbound through a same-day response workflow.
- Fast scheduling and high-signal first screen to prevent drop-off.

## 3) Passive Candidate Outreach Copy Templates

Use these as templates; customize the bracketed fields in every send.

What may be relevant for you:
- Direct influence on roadmap and architecture from day one
- Tight build/measure/ship loop with founders
- Meaningful equity in an early, high-leverage role

Open to a 15-minute exploratory chat this week or next?

— [Name]  
[Role] | [Startup]  
[Scheduling link]

Would a quick intro call be worth it?

— [Name]

Either way, appreciate your time.

— [Name]

## 4) Funnel Metrics (Weekly) and Stage Targets

Track weekly and cumulative metrics in a shared sheet/ATS.

| Funnel stage | Definition | Weekly target | Owner |
|---|---|---:|---|
| New prospects added | Net new qualified profiles sourced | 80 | Recruiting/CMO |
| Outreach sent | First-touch messages sent | 60 | Recruiting/CMO |
| Response rate | Responses / outreach sent | >=25% | Recruiting/CMO |
| Qualified screens booked | Candidate meets baseline and books intro | 10-12 | Recruiting/CMO |
| CEO/CTO first calls completed | Intro calls finished | 8-10 | CEO/CTO |
| Technical loop completed | Candidates complete agreed interview loop | 3-4 | CTO |
| Finalist slate | Candidates ready for founder decision | 2-3 | CEO/CTO |
| Offer extended | Formal offer sent | 1 | CEO |
| Offer accepted | Candidate accepts | 1 | CEO |

**Quality guardrail metric:** >=70% of screened candidates should meet scorecard baseline.  
If below 70%, tighten sourcing filters and message positioning within 1 week.

## 5) Weekly Operating Cadence (with CEO/CTO Touchpoints)

## Monday (30 min): Funnel + calibration sync
- Participants: CMO/recruiting, CEO, CTO
- Review prior-week metrics and stage conversion by channel.
- Recalibrate profile targeting and outreach narrative.
- Confirm top 15 priority prospects for founder-level outreach.

## Tuesday/Thursday (20 min each): Candidate standup
- Participants: CMO/recruiting + CTO (CEO optional on Tue, required Thu)
- Review active candidates, response bottlenecks, scheduling risk.
- Decide same-day actions (follow-up, fast-track, or close).

## Friday (20 min): Founder debrief + decision checkpoint
- Participants: CEO + CTO + CMO/recruiting
- Review candidate scorecards and interview signal quality.
- Decide: advance, hold, reject, or prep offer.
- Ensure all candidate comms are sent before end of day.

**Operating SLA commitments:**
- Inbound response within 24 hours.
- Post-interview feedback submitted within 12 hours.
- Candidate next-step communication within 24 hours.

## 6) Dependencies on CTO Hiring Package Inputs

This plan depends on receiving the following CTO inputs before full-scale launch:

1. **Role scorecard (must-have vs nice-to-have)**  
   Needed for sourcing filters and screen qualification.
2. **Technical scope brief (next 12 months)**  
   Needed for accurate candidate pitch and expectation setting.
3. **Interview loop and rubric**  
   Stages, interviewers, evaluation dimensions, pass/fail criteria.
4. **Compensation and equity band**  
   Required to avoid late-stage drop-off and misalignment.
5. **Tech stack and architecture snapshot**  
   Helps attract relevant profiles and establish credibility.
6. **Non-negotiables and risk flags**  
   Defines automatic no-go criteria early in funnel.
